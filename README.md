The product pages themselves reveal several restrictions that are easy to miss when a coupon article focuses only on the headline price.

**Some products restrict usage.** The U.S. household-IP VDS page explicitly prohibits activities that could generate IP complaints, including spam, bulk mailing, attacks, scanning, phishing/fraud and resource abuse.

**Some products have special refund rules.** As noted above, “48-hour refund” does not apply identically across the catalog. Some special products are balance-only refunds and some have no refunds.

**Some products are not designed for long-term bandwidth saturation.** The U.S. residential CN2 NAT product explicitly describes peak bandwidth and says prolonged occupation is prohibited.

**Certain IP segments may behave differently from ordinary VPS addresses.** The current U.S. 9929 page notes that some IP ranges have ping disabled at the provider's request. A failed ping therefore does not necessarily mean the server is offline.

These are precisely the details that are more useful than another paragraph saying a VPS is “fast.”
